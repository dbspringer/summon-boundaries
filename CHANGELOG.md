# Changelog

## 1

First release, for WoW: Forever (the 1.60 client).

- Type `/sb` to see your settings and the reply strangers will get
- On a warlock, answers a stranger's summon request with one firm reply, at most once per cooldown for each player. Your party, raid, friends, guild, and anyone you've just whispered never get it.
- A warlock who hasn't learned Ritual of Summoning yet says so, rather than asking for clickers
- Recognizes "smn", "sumon", "lock port", and "warlock port" as summon requests too
- Auto-replies start off. Type `/sb on` to turn them on, and `/sb off` to stop.
- Change your reply with `/sb message`, the fee with `/sb fee`, and how long each player waits for another reply with `/sb cooldown`
- `/sb test` shows your reply without sending it, and `/sb help` lists every command
